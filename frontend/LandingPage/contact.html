Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with TX RetailOS?

▼

Getting started is easy! Request a demo through our website, and our team will guide you through the onboarding process. We provide comprehensive training and support to ensure a smooth transition.

What is the pricing model for TX RetailOS?

▼

We offer flexible pricing plans tailored to businesses of all sizes. Pricing is based on the number of stores and users. Contact our sales team for a customized quote.

Does TX RetailOS work without internet?

▼

Yes! TX RetailOS is built with offline-first architecture. You can process sales, manage inventory, and run your business even without internet connectivity. Data automatically syncs when you're back online.

What kind of support do you provide?

▼

We provide 24/7 customer support through email, phone, and WhatsApp. Our support team is trained to help with technical issues, feature questions, and general guidance.

Can I integrate TX RetailOS with my existing systems?

▼

Yes, TX RetailOS supports integrations with various payment gateways, accounting software, and other business tools. Contact our team to discuss your specific integration needs.

Is my data secure with TX RetailOS?

▼

Absolutely. We employ industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your data. All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest, and we comply with international data protection regulations.
