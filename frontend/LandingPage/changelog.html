v2.3.0 July 1, 2026 Major Release

Introducing advanced analytics dashboard and improved offline sync performance. This release includes significant improvements to data synchronization and reporting capabilities.

✨
Advanced Analytics Dashboard: New real-time analytics dashboard with customizable charts and KPI tracking for sales, inventory, and customer insights.
⚡
Improved Sync Performance: Optimized synchronization engine reduces sync time by 40% and improves battery efficiency on mobile devices.
