RetailCo: From Downtime to Dominance with TX RetailOS

Business Profile: RetailCo is a rapidly expanding supermarket chain operating in several towns across Kenya, with a focus on providing affordable groceries and household items to local communities. Before TX RetailOS, their biggest challenge was maintaining consistent sales and inventory records due to intermittent internet access, leading to lost sales and customer frustration.

The Challenge

  • Frequent internet disruptions caused POS systems to crash, halting sales.
  • Manual reconciliation of sales data after outages was time-consuming and error-prone.
  • Inaccurate inventory counts led to stockouts and overstocking.

The Solution: Implementing TX RetailOS

RetailCo adopted TX RetailOS for its robust offline capabilities and comprehensive retail management features. The transition was seamless, with minimal disruption to daily operations. Our team provided on-site training and continuous support.
