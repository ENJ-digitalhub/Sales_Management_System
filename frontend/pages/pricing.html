Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We've got answers.

Can I change my plan anytime?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle, and we'll prorate any differences.
Is there a setup fee?
No setup fees! We believe in transparent pricing. You only pay the monthly subscription amount. Some customers opt for paid onboarding support, but it's completely optional.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept M-Pesa, bank transfers, and major credit cards. For Enterprise customers, we can arrange custom payment terms.
Do you offer discounts for annual billing?
Yes! Annual billing saves you 20% compared to monthly pricing. That's 2.4 months free when you commit to a year.
Is there a free trial?
Absolutely! All plans come with a 30-day free trial. No credit card required to get started. After the trial, you'll be charged only if you choose to continue.
What happens if I cancel?
You can cancel anytime with no penalties. Your data remains accessible for 30 days, giving you time to export or migrate if needed.
