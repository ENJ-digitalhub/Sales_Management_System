Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small pieces of data stored on your device (computer or mobile device) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

2. How We Use Cookies

TX RetailOS uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility.
  • Performance Cookies: These cookies collect information about how you use our website, such as which pages you visit most often and whether you receive error messages.
  • Functional Cookies: These cookies remember your preferences and choices to provide a personalized experience.
  • Marketing Cookies: These cookies are used to track your activity across websites to display targeted advertisements.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Session Cookies: These are temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: These cookies remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.
  • Third-Party Cookies: These are set by domains other than the one you are visiting.

4. Your Cookie Choices

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish. You can delete all cookies that are already on your computer and you can set most browsers to prevent them from being placed. If you do this, however, you may have to manually adjust some preferences every time you visit a website and some services and functionalities may not work.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at privacy@txretailos.com
